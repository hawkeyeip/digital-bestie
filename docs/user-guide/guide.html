Chapter 01

🛡️ Welcome & The Sovereign Privacy Promise

Digital Bestie is designed with a fundamental architectural principle: your thoughts, financials, passwords, and emotional reflections belong solely to you.

Unlike cloud-hosted AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) where your prompts and files are transmitted to corporate data centers and stored on remote servers, Digital Bestie runs entirely on your Mac's Apple Silicon Unified Memory using Ollama. No remote servers ever read your chats, living dossier, or memory logs.

The 100% Local-First Promise: All user data lives inside ~/.digital-bestie/ on your computer. When encrypted via the macOS Keychain (Electron safeStorage), the files are inaccessible to unauthorized processes.

Chapter 02

💻 Installation & Hardware Initialization

Digital Bestie is distributed as a signed macOS Universal DMG for Apple Silicon Macs (M1, M2, M3, M4).

Step 1: Install Application

Download Digital Bestie-darwin-arm64-2.5.0.dmg from GitHub Releases. Drag Digital Bestie.app into your /Applications folder.

macOS Gatekeeper Verification: If macOS displays an unidentified developer warning on first launch, open Terminal and execute: 
xattr -cr "/Applications/Digital Bestie.app"

Step 2: Recommended Starter Models

Make sure Ollama is installed and running on your machine. Open Terminal and pull the recommended weights according to your Mac's Unified Memory:

🚀 Mac Studio Tier (64GB+ RAM)

ollama pull hermes3:70b
ollama pull qwen2.5-coder:32b
ollama pull nomic-embed-text

Highest reasoning fidelity for executive strategy and full-stack software development.

⚡ MacBook Pro / Air (16GB–36GB RAM)

ollama pull qwen2.5:14b
ollama pull llama3.1:8b
ollama pull nomic-embed-text

Instant token throughput, ultra-low memory footprint, balanced performance.

🛡️ Vault Backup, Disaster Recovery & Migration

Because Digital Bestie is 100% local-first, protecting your data against a lost, stolen, or damaged Mac requires an off-device backup strategy.

What Happens if You Uninstall Digital Bestie? Dragging Digital Bestie.app to the Trash DOES NOT delete your data! macOS only deletes the app binary. All your profile data, Living Dossier, credentials, memories, chats, and configurations live in ~/.digital-bestie/ and remain safe on disk. Reinstalling reconnects immediately.

The Three Defense Layers:

  1. Daily Local Snapshots (7-Day Rolling): Automatically created once per calendar day on app launch and stored in ~/.digital-bestie/snapshots/. Protects against accidental deletions.
  2. Encrypted Portable Exports (.bestie-backup): Military-grade AES-256-GCM encrypted backup protected by PBKDF2-SHA256 (310,000 iterations). Machine-bound macOS Keychain tokens are decrypted before packaging and re-encrypted with the new Mac's Keychain upon restore.
  3. Automatic Off-Device Mirroring (iCloud Drive / Dropbox / USB): Enable in Settings > Backup & Recovery. Bestie will silently generate encrypted copies into your chosen sync folder whenever milestones are reached.

Migrating to a Brand-New Mac:

1. Export a .bestie-backup file on your old Mac.
2. On your new Mac, open Digital Bestie.
3. On the welcome screen, click "Restore from backup 📦".
4. Enter your passphrase. Your entire profile, credentials, and chats are instantly restored!

Digital Bestie features an automated background updater and self-healing intelligence engine:

  • Automatic Update Notifications: Polls GitHub Releases every 30 minutes. When a new version is published, a glowing topbar badge and banner alert appear. Click to review improvements and download the DMG.
  • Self-Healing Dynamic Fallback: If your active model is deleted or Ollama runs out of memory, the engine seamlessly fails over (bestie-abliterated → hermes3:70b → bestie-light → qwen2.5:14b → any available model) rather than crashing.
